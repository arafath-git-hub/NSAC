A CATALOGUE OF EXPLORATION

Built for a mission.
Remembered by a world.

Every artifact in this archive began as a practical part of a mission: a vehicle to travel, an instrument to measure, a structure to land, or a tool to help a crew work. Some objects completed their tasks exactly as planned. Others outlived expectations or stopped working in distant places.

This archive groups notable examples by mission and destination. Active spacecraft are identified as active rather than described as abandoned.

A NOTE ON THE ARCHIVE

Not every object is abandoned.

Some spacecraft listed in the wider AFTERLIGHT story are still operating. The archive distinguishes completed missions from active missions so that the history remains accurate. Curiosity and Perseverance, for example, are active Mars rover missions and are included as part of the ongoing exploration story — not as abandoned equipment.
