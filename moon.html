01 — THE BEGINNING

A SMALL STEP.
AN ENDURING PRESENCE.

When Apollo 11 landed on the Moon in 1969, humanity made history. But the mission left behind more than footprints.

Cameras, scientific instruments, tools, and parts of spacecraft remained on the lunar surface. They became silent reminders of one of humanity's greatest journeys.

1959

Luna 2

The Soviet spacecraft became the first human-made object to reach the Moon.

1969

Apollo 11

Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin became the first humans to walk on the Moon.

1971

Apollo 15

The first crewed lunar rover mission expanded exploration across the surface.

1972

Apollo 17

The final crewed Apollo Moon landing left behind another chapter of humanity's lunar history.
